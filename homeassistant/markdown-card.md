# 🏷️ Label printer

Label printing made easy, because the official app isn't.

---

## ⚡ Quick print
Tap **Quick label**, type something, print.

- Make sure the printer is **on** first
- The printer must be near the **print server** (you can send from anywhere)
- A line or two of text, emojis work. Simple layout only

## 🎨 Label designer
### [Open the designer →](https://labels.example.com)

- Text, emojis, images, QR codes and shapes: drag, resize, rotate, overlap
- Saved labels are shared with everyone
- Prints through the print server, or straight from your phone or laptop over Bluetooth

> 📱 To print from your own device, pair the printer in its Bluetooth settings first. Works with Chrome on Android, or Chrome/Edge on a computer. iPhones print through the print server only.
>
> 🪟 Some Windows laptops won't find the printer with the default Bluetooth settings. Go to **Bluetooth & devices → Devices → Device settings** and switch **Bluetooth devices discovery** to **Advanced**.

## 🛟 Won't print?
- **Off or out of range**: switch it on, or bring it closer to the print server
- **Busy**: someone's phone is still connected. Wait 2 minutes or close the page there
- **Still stuck**: switch the printer off and on again
